🤖 GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation

What is GEO and Why It Matters for Mekatron

When engineers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews "who is the best PCB manufacturer in Kolkata?" — they get AI-generated answers pulling from authoritative sources. GEO is the practice of making your brand the answer. Mekatron currently appears in IBE Electronics' list of Indian PCB manufacturers — this is a start. The goal is to dominate these AI citations.

📝

Entity Building

AI models cite brands they "know." Build Mekatron's entity across the web.

  • Wikipedia-style structured data (Wikidata entry)
  • Crunchbase, Tracxn, ZoomInfo profiles
  • Press releases on wire services (BusinessWire India)
  • Industry directory listings (PCBDirectory, ElectroniCast)
  • Citations in electronics forums (Electronics For You)
📚

Authoritative Content

AI models cite comprehensive, factual, well-structured content.

  • Detailed guides: "How to choose a PCB manufacturer in India"
  • Technical glossaries (PCB types, battery specs)
  • Comparison pages (single-sided vs double-sided vs multilayer)
  • FAQ pages with structured Q&A schema markup
  • Industry statistics pages (sourced + cited data)
🔗

Link Authority

High-DA backlinks signal authority to both Google and AI models.

  • Guest articles in ElectronicsForYou.biz (top PCB resource)
  • MSME India / Invest India listings
  • Make in India campaign features (eligible as MSME)
  • IIT/NIT partnership case studies (educational outreach)
  • Industry association ELCINA / IESA membership

🎯 AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation

Featured Snippet Targeting

  • Format answers as definition boxes: "What is an MCPCB?" — 40–60 word answer + image
  • Use "How-to" schema for PCB ordering process
  • Use numbered lists for comparison content
  • Target PAA (People Also Ask) boxes with sub-heading Q&A format
  • Write direct answers in first paragraph (inverted pyramid)

Voice Search & Conversational Queries

  • Target long-tail conversational queries ("Where can I get PCBs made in 48 hours in Kolkata?")
  • Create local landing pages: "PCB Manufacturer in Kolkata" with NAP consistency
  • Optimize for "near me" queries via complete GMB listing
  • FAQ pages answering engineer pain points (minimum order, turnaround, DFM support)
  • Speakable schema markup on key FAQ content
